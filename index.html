More About Me

Hi, my name is Shanice Webber!

After spending a few years working in warehouses and gaining some office and customer service experience, I decided to pivot into the tech world. I will be completing my studies in computer programming in 2026, and I'm excited about where this new path will take me.

Working in fast-paced environments taught me how to stay organized, adapt quickly, and tackle problems head-on. Whether it was managing inventory efficiently or juggling multiple tasks in an office setting, I developed a strong work ethic and an eye for detail. These skills naturally carry over to programming, where problem solving and precision are key.

In my coding journey, I've been focusing on learning the basics of different coding languages, from C# to Python, to HTML, combining my hands-on approach from past roles with new technical knowledge. I'm passionate about creating solutions that make a difference and always eager to learn and grow.

I'm hoping to find myself in a software development role, or any other role that involves coding. That's what I like to do the most. Thanks for stopping by to learn a bit about me. I'm looking forward to new opportunities and collaborations in the tech world!
